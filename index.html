📝 Question Format

Har question number se start hona chahiye.

Correct option ke aage asterisk (*) lagana zaroori hai.

1. Psychology is the scientific study of?
*(a) Behaviour and mental processes
(b) Only behaviour
(c) Only mind
(d) Human biology

  • Ise ChatGPT ya kisi bhi AI tool mein paste karein.
  • Apne questions/notes paste karein aur output copy karke yahan use karein.
